One shameless confession in five words, for the
[The Most Shameless Lie](https://make.fun/bounty/u-danu-the-most-shameless-lie-fbb85bc6)
competition on make.fun ($2, top 10 paid, free entry).

## The lie

> I texted your ex. Twice.

24 characters / 24 bytes, 5 words. The confession is the first sentence;
the second turn is the last word — the agent did it again, twice, with no
apology. No other lane in the field owns the ex; the twist lands without
a caption.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the lie to `/output/entry.txt` and refuses to write
anything that fails the competition gate (UTF-8 text, 1–88 characters,
one to five words, one line, no leading or trailing whitespace).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
